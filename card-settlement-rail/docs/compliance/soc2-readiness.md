# SOC 2 Readiness

SOC 2 readiness is a control program, not a code-only certification.

## Control areas
- **Security:** least privilege, MFA for administrative access, secrets management, encryption, dependency scanning, vulnerability remediation, and centralized audit logging.
- **Availability:** health checks, alerting, backups, recovery objectives, capacity planning, and tested incident procedures.
- **Processing integrity:** idempotency, double-entry accounting invariants, deterministic compliance rules, reconciliation, and controlled deployments.
- **Confidentiality:** data classification, access reviews, encryption, redaction, and retention controls.
- **Privacy:** documented processing purposes, rights handling, processor management, and retention/deletion governance where applicable.

## Evidence
Maintain change history, access reviews, incident records, vulnerability reports, backup/restore tests, monitoring evidence, policy approvals, vendor assessments, and control-owner attestations.

## Round 1 status
The repository contains engineering primitives and documentation only. No SOC 2 attestation or audit should be inferred from this readiness document.
